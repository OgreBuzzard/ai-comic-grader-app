What RoboGrader is

RoboGrader is an informational tool. It looks at photos of comic books and produces grade estimates and defect descriptions. We make a best-effort attempt at accuracy, but the grades RoboGrader produces are not official grades from CGC, PSA, or any other grading service. They are software-generated estimates based on photos.

Not a substitute for professional grading

RoboGrader grades are for your own use — tracking your collection, deciding whether to submit a book for professional grading, or just seeing what software thinks. They are not authoritative. Do not rely on RoboGrader grades for:

  • Buying or selling decisions where you're representing the book's condition to another party
  • Insurance valuations
  • Legal disputes about a book's condition
  • Any situation where a professional grade is expected

RoboGrader cannot prevent this and is not responsible for any misuse of printed labels.

If you receive a label with a comic — whether in person, by mail, or otherwise — verify that the photos on the verification page actually match the physical book in front of you. The QR code on every label links to the verification page. Buyers should always check.
