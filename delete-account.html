Delete Your Account

How to delete your Robograder account and data

Robograder is a comic book grading app operated by ROBOGRADER, LLC. This page explains how to request deletion of your Robograder account and the data associated with it.

How to delete your account

You can delete your account and all associated data directly in the app at any time:

  1. Open the Robograder app and sign in.
  2. Tap the gear (Settings) icon in the top-right corner.
  3. Scroll to Account Actions and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when prompted. Your account and data are permanently deleted immediately.

If you are unable to access the app, you can instead email support@robograder.app from the email address associated with your account and request deletion. We will process the request and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account and profile information (name and email address)
  • All of your saved comics and the details you entered (titles, issues, grades, notes, prices)
  • All photos you uploaded
  • Your assessment history and grades
  • Any remaining assessment credits

This action is irreversible.

What may be kept, and for how long

After account deletion, a limited set of records may be retained only where necessary:

  • Purchase and transaction records may be retained where required for legal, tax, accounting, and fraud-prevention purposes.
  • Anonymized assessment data that had already been incorporated into grading-model improvement work may persist, but it carries no link to your name, email, or account, so it cannot be traced back to you.
  • Short-term server logs (for example, our hosting provider's security and debugging logs) may persist for a brief period before automatic expiry.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

You do not have to delete your whole account to remove data. You can delete individual comics — including their photos and details — from within the app at any time using the delete option on each comic's detail page.

Questions? Email support@robograder.app.
